Privacy

Lineform for macOS

Lineform is local-first. Your writing stays on your device.

  • No account system.
  • No analytics collection by default.
  • No document upload.

What leaves your device

Your documents never leave your Mac except in these two cases:

  • Update checks. Release builds check for a newer version using Sparkle. This sends only the information needed to check for an update — never your document content.
  • Announcement checks. Once a day Lineform reads a small public file on the Lineform website to see whether there is any news, such as a new version. The request sends no account, no identifier, and no information about you, your Mac, or your documents, and nothing about the check is stored. You can turn it off in Settings, and when it is off Lineform makes no network request at all.

Local files

Text you pipe into the lineform command line tool is written to a local file in ~/Library/Application Support/Lineform/Piped/ and opened as a normal document. These files stay on your device and are automatically deleted after about 7 days of inactivity.

If a diagram fails to render, a local diagram log is kept on your device to help with triage. It never leaves your Mac — there is no feature that sends it, and no feature in Lineform transmits your document content anywhere.

Your documents

Documents are ordinary Markdown or text files. They stay wherever you save them, and remain portable across Finder, iCloud Drive, Git, and other editors.
