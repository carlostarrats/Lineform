Privacy

Lineform for macOS

Lineform is local-first. Your writing stays on your device.

  • No account system.
  • No analytics collection by default.
  • No document upload.

What leaves your device

Lineform's developer never receives your documents or anything you write. Lineform itself makes a network request in only these three cases (links you choose to open go to your web browser):

  • AI, only if you connect one. AI is off until you choose Connect Your AI (Lineform ▸ Connect Your AI… on Mac, Menu ▸ Settings on iPhone) and connect a provider with your own key. Text is sent only when you choose an AI action, and only the prose of the passage you select, or the whole document when you choose Ask AI for the whole document, goes, with any comment you type, straight from your device to the provider you connected. For Find, that is each whole sentence your selection touches, or every sentence of the document. Its file name is never sent. Your key stays in your device's Keychain.
  • Check for Updates. When you choose Check for Updates… (Lineform ▸ Check for Updates… on Mac, Menu ▸ Settings on iPhone), Lineform asks Apple's App Store for the current version of Lineform. The request names the app and adds no account, no identifier, and nothing about you or your documents. Nothing about the check is stored, and it happens only when you choose the command.
  • Announcement checks. Once a day Lineform reads a small public file on the Lineform website to see whether there is any news, such as a new version. Lineform adds no account, no identifier, and nothing about you or your documents to the request. On your device it keeps only the time of the last check, a copy of the public file, and which announcements you dismissed. You can turn it off in Settings, and when it is off Lineform makes no automatic network request at all.

Like any request from your device, these reach the server with your IP address and the standard details your Mac or iPhone includes (the app's build, the macOS or iOS version, and your preferred languages). Lineform's developer does not collect or keep them.

Local files

History (the versions you keep with Replace With…) is stored only on your Mac, inside Lineform's own folder. It is never written into your Markdown file and never sent anywhere.

If a diagram fails to render, a local diagram log is kept on your device. It never leaves your Mac — there is no feature that sends it.

Your documents

Documents are ordinary Markdown or text files. They stay wherever you save them, and remain portable across Finder, iCloud Drive, Git, and other editors.
